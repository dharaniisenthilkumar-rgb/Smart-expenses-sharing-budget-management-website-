1. PROJECT NAME 
 SMART EXPENSES SHARING BUDGET MANAGEMENT 
2. Problem Statement
Students and groups often share expenses during trips, accommodation, food, transportation, and events. Manually calculating individual shares can lead to calculation errors, confusion about who owes whom, and difficulty tracking the overall budget.
Existing methods such as notebooks, calculators, and chat messages do not provide a centralized way to record expenses, calculate settlements, monitor budgets, and analyze spending.
Therefore, we propose SmartSplit, a web application that automatically manages shared expenses, calculates member-wise balances, determines who owes whom, tracks budgets, and provides spending insights through interactive charts.
